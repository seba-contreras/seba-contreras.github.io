Research

I am a complex-systems physicist working at the boundary between statistical physics and epidemiology. Using mathematical modelling, I search for general principles in the dynamics of infectious diseases and for quantitative insight into the controllability of outbreaks — with a focus on systems that adapt to what is spreading in them: behaviour that reacts to risk, testing that reacts to prevalence, and contact networks that rewire under both.
